Bank statement data conversion

Convert bank and credit-card statements to reviewed Excel, CSV, or QIF data.

More than a raw document export

Troppe Financial Data Services converts digital PDFs and scanned bank or credit-card statements into structured transaction data. Each project follows an agreed field layout and output format.

Transaction schedules can include date, description, debit, credit, amount, and account fields. Uncertain entries are placed on an exception schedule, and delivered totals are checked against the source statements.

The result is prepared source data for your professional workflow. It is not bookkeeping, account reconciliation, transaction categorization, tax work, legal analysis, or financial advice.

Available outputs
  • Excel transaction schedules
  • CSV transaction data
  • Quicken/QIF import files
  • Exception schedules
  • Reconciliation summaries
