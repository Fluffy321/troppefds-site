Business valuation support

Turn statement backlogs into data your valuation team can review.

Keep professional time focused on valuation work

When an engagement includes months of bank and credit-card statements, manual transaction entry can consume analyst time before the substantive work begins. Troppe Financial Data Services prepares the statement activity into consistent, reviewed schedules.

The delivery can include Excel or CSV transaction data, QIF files for compatible workflows, documented exceptions, and statement-level reconciliation checks.

Jonathan’s relevant experience comes from supporting a valuation firm on statement-heavy cases. The service does not perform valuation analysis, accounting judgments, or financial conclusions.

Useful for
  • Statement-heavy valuation matters
  • Historical transaction schedules
  • Bank and card activity organization
  • Multiple-account projects
  • Scanned or inconsistent files

Clear boundaries

Preparation stops where professional judgment begins.

  • Organized source data

    Transactions are arranged into the fields and file format agreed before work begins.

  • Uncertainty documented

    Ambiguous descriptions or unreadable entries are flagged for the valuation team’s review.

  • Totals checked

    Delivered schedules are compared with the statement’s balance activity.

  • No valuation conclusions

    Your professionals retain all classification, normalization, analysis, and opinion work.
