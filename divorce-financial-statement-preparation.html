Divorce-finance support

Organize years of statements before marital financial analysis.

Reviewed schedules for statement-heavy matters

Divorce-finance work can arrive as years of statements across several bank and credit-card accounts. Troppe Financial Data Services converts those records into structured transaction data before the professional analysis begins.

Deliverables can include an Excel or CSV transaction schedule, QIF files when requested, an exception list for uncertain entries, and reconciliation checks against the source statements.

The service does not classify marital or separate property, make tracing decisions, calculate lifestyle conclusions, or provide legal, tax, accounting, or expert opinions. Those decisions remain with your team.

Useful for
  • Marital financial analysis preparation
  • Lifestyle-review data organization
  • Asset-tracing source schedules
  • Multiple accounts and long periods
  • Scanned and older statements

Confidential workflow

Scope and secure transfer come first.

  • Initial description

    Start with approximate accounts, months, file types, desired output, and deadline—not the records themselves.

  • Written scope

    The work product, price, schedule, responsibilities, and boundaries are agreed before work begins.

  • Secure transfer

    A restricted transfer method is confirmed before unredacted financial records move.

  • Documented delivery

    Prepared data, exceptions, and reconciliation checks are returned together on the agreed schedule.
